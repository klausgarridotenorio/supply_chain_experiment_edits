How to Use the Interface

📋 Official vs. Non-Official Agreements

Chat messages are non-official. Only offers confirmed through the interface are official.


✅ Finalizing Your Deal

Once you reach an agreement, click "CONFIRM" to finalize the negotiation.

📤 Sending Official Offers

Use "Your Current Proposal" to send official offers.

IMPORTANT when the 5 min countdown is below 30 seconds you can re-set it back to 30 seconds by sending an official offer.
