Official vs. Non-Official Agreements

Chat messages are non-official. Only offers confirmed through the interface are official.


Finalizing Your Deal

Once you reach an agreement, click "CONFIRM" to finalize the negotiation.

{% if player.bot_opponent %}
  • The button appears when your counterpart is ready
  • AI Agents can automatically accept official offers
{% endif %}
Sending Official Offers

Use "Your Current Proposal" to send official offers.

IMPORTANT when the countdown is below 30 seconds you can re-set it back to 30 seconds by sending an official offer.

{% if player.is_retailer %}

Important: Don't accept a wholesale price above €{% group.market_price %}, or you won't make a profit.

{% endif %}
On This Screen
  • Use the chat below to negotiate with your counterpart, and the Interface under it to send/receive official offers
  • Use the Decision Support System on the right to test Wholesale Price and Quantity combinations before sending an offer
  • Click TEST offer (next to CONFIRM) to preview the average profitability of the deal currently on the table
