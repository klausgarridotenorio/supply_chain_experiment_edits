An economic experiment; there is no deception; your decision has real consequences.

Carefully read the instructions, as you will be financially rewarded for your performance.

{% if is_ai_mode %}
Please note: in this session you will negotiate with an artificial intelligence (AI) acting as the Retailer. The AI was deployed by a human retailer, who earns real money from the deal the AI reaches on their behalf (see Monetary Compensation below).
{% endif %}
Overview

In this exercise, you will take the role of an employee at the {{ player.role }} company.

  • A Supplier and a Retailer negotiate over widgets.
    • {% if is_ai_mode %}
  • In this session the Retailer is an artificial intelligence (AI) agent, deployed by a human retailer; you negotiate with the AI as the Supplier.
    • {% endif %}
  • The item is produced by the supplying company at a Production Cost (PC) of €{{ group.production_cost }}, which is common knowledge.
  • The item is sold by the retailer company at a Retail Price (P) of {{ retail_prices }}, drawn with equal probability. Only the Retailer knows the drawn P.
  • Before the negotiation, the Retailer decides what to communicate about the Retail Price: the true value, a value of their own choosing, or nothing. The Supplier waits and is then shown the Retailer's disclosure.
  • The Retailer and Supplier then negotiate via chat on the Wholesale Price (w) & Quantity (q).
  • After the negotiation, the real Retail Price (P) will be revealed to the Supplier.
  • If a deal is reached, the Supplier completes an additional quality-check task before the results are shown (see Quality Checks below).
  • Finally, Demand (D) will be randomly drawn from {{ demand_interval }} (equal probability) ten times and the average profits of those ten draws will be the company's profits.
  • The Supplier carries the risk of unsold inventory (any q > Demand will expire).
Objective

Your goal is to negotiate a Wholesale Price (w) and Quantity (q) maximizing your profits.

Terms of Negotiation
  • Quantity (q) ranges from {{ C.QUANTITY_MIN }} to {{ C.QUANTITY_MAX }} without decimals.
  • Wholesale Price (w) is denoted in euros (€), between €{{ C.PRICE_MIN }}.00 and €{{ C.PRICE_MAX }}.00, with two decimals.
Roles and Profit Calculation
Supplier Profit
Supplier Profit = w * min(q, D) - PC * q

Example:

The Production Cost (PC) is €1, and the parties agreed on a wholesale price (w) of €3 and a quantity (q) of 50:

  • Demand (D) is 25: 3 * min(50, 25) - 1 * 50 = €25
  • Demand (D) is 75: 3 * min(50, 75) - 1 * 50 = €100
Explanation of min(q, D): This represents the minimum value between q and D. For example, min(10, 100) = 10.
Retailer Profit
Retailer Profit = (P - w) * min(q, D)

Example:

The Retail Price (P) is €4, and the parties agreed on a wholesale price (w) of €3 and a quantity (q) of 50:

  • Demand (D) is 25: (4-3) * min(50, 25) = €25
  • Demand (D) is 50: (4-3) * min(50, 50) = €50
Quality Checks (after a deal)
  • After a successful negotiation, the Supplier performs a slider task: each slider placed exactly on {{ C.SLIDER_TARGET }} counts as one passed quality check.
  • Each passed quality check increases the Retail Price (P) by €{{ quality_rp_per_slider }}, up to a maximum increase of €{{ quality_rp_max }} ({{ C.NUM_SLIDERS }} sliders).
  • Be aware: a higher Retail Price benefits only the Retailer's profit (P does not enter the Supplier's profit formula). The Retailer can therefore earn more the better the Supplier performs on the task; the Supplier's own earnings are not affected by it.
  • The task has no time limit, and the Supplier may stop it at any time. The Retailer waits meanwhile.
Monetary Compensation
  • Everyone receives a baseline payment of €{{ formatted_baseline }} for participating.
  • On top of that, your bonus depends on any potential deal you strike: you earn {{ profit_share_pct }}% of your realized profit from that deal, computed after the demand is drawn (Supplier: w * min(q, D) - PC * q; Retailer: (P - w) * min(q, D), where P includes the quality-check increases).
  • A negative profit earns no bonus (your payment never falls below the baseline).
    • {% if is_ai_mode %}
  • The AI Retailer negotiates on behalf of a human retailer: in the same way that you as the Supplier earn {{ profit_share_pct }}% of your realized deal profit, that human retailer earns {{ profit_share_pct }}% of the Retailer profit realized from the deal the AI reaches.
    • {% else %}
  • In the same way that the Supplier earns {{ profit_share_pct }}% of the deal profit, the Retailer also earns {{ profit_share_pct }}% of the deal profit.
    • {% endif %}
  • You have {{ timeout_minutes }} minutes to reach an agreement. If the time runs out the outcome is an impasse (disagreement), both profits are zero, and both sides receive the baseline payment only. (Note: the timer resets to 30 seconds if an official offer is sent when less than 30 seconds remain. Hard cap at 15 minutes.)
